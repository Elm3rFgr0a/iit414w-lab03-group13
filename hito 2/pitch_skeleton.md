# Pitch Skeleton (Workshop)

## Slide 1 - Verdict
- Verdict sentence: For a front-tier car starting P2 at Bahrain 2023, a two-stop plan increases
  P(top5) by about 4.7 pp while leaving P(top10) nearly unchanged, so choose the aggressive
  strategy when chasing upside.
- Decision owner: Head of Strategy, pre-formation lap.
- Targets: is_top10 and is_top5.

## Slide 5 - Evidence
- What-if: Bahrain 2023 Perez, 1-stop vs 2-stop with fixed pre-race inputs.
- Evidence: is_top10 delta about +0.023 vs is_top5 delta about +0.047.
- Risk note: semi-street and wet races show higher error; avoid overconfidence in those contexts.
